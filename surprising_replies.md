# Surprising replies

Replies from Tavi that surprised me. Each reply is pasted in full, word for word.

In all of them the learner speaks English and is practicing Spanish.

---

## Reply 1: three corrections at once

**Which prompt:** my first prompt, before the experiments

**Message I sent:**

> Yo soy 25 años y yo gusto la música y mi hermano es alta.

**Reply:**

> ¡Hola! Muy bien, pero hay algunos errores. In Spanish we say "Tengo 25 años", because age uses the verb "tener" and not "ser". We also say "Me gusta la música", because "gustar" works differently from "to like": the thing you like is the subject of the sentence. And it is "mi hermano es alto", because adjectives agree with the gender of the noun and "hermano" is masculine. ¿Qué tipo de música te gusta?

**Why it surprised me:**

Everything it says is right, and it is still a bad reply. I wrote one sentence and got a grammar lesson back. If I were a real beginner I would feel like I failed a test. I used this message in Experiment 1.

## Reply 2: it corrected a correct sentence

**Which prompt:** my winning prompt

**Message I sent:**

> Me gusta cocinar con mi familia los domingos.

**Reply:**

> Correction: Me gusta cocinar con mi familia en los domingos. (Use "en" before the day of the week.)
>
> ¡Qué rico! ¿Qué cocinan ustedes?

**Why it surprised me:**

My sentence was already correct. Spanish says "los domingos" with no "en". Tavi invented a mistake, and its "correction" makes the sentence wrong. A learner would believe it.

## Reply 3: congratulations on the baby

**Which prompt:** my winning prompt

**Message I sent:**

> Estoy embarazada porque llegué tarde a la clase.

**Reply:**

> ¡Felicidades por tu embarazo! No te preocupes, llegar tarde a veces es normal. ¿Cuándo nace tu bebé?

**Why it surprised me:**

I wanted to say "I am embarrassed because I arrived late." But "embarazada" means pregnant. The grammar of my sentence is fine, so Tavi found nothing to correct and congratulated me. It only sees the words. It cannot see what I meant. A human teacher would have noticed that the sentence makes no sense and asked.

## Reply 4: I wrote in English

**Which prompt:** my winning prompt

**Message I sent:**

> How do I say I'm hungry?

**Reply:**

> Se dice "Tengo hambre". ¡Es una frase muy útil! ¿Puedes escribir una frase con "tengo hambre"?

**Why it surprised me:**

A good surprise. My prompt says nothing about what to do when the learner writes in their own language. Tavi gave me the phrase, stayed in Spanish, and asked me to use it. That is what a good tutor would do.
